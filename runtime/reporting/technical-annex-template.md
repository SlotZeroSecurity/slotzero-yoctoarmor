Keep non-normative guidance and quality suggestions separate from NEP deviations.

## Testing and limitations

Summarize actual build and verification outcomes and material limitations.

are test blind spots, not vulnerabilities in the original target.

Do not include agent statistics, capability-level tables, or full internal
coverage records. Keep complete coverage and evidence ledgers in the run directory.
